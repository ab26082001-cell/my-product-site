The problem

Preparation is scattered. Readiness is invisible.

Every placement cycle, students rebuild the same knowledge from scratch, and most of it disappears the moment a batch graduates.

01 · MATERIAL

Knowledge is scattered

Notes live across senior drives, WhatsApp groups and one-off PDFs, with no shared structure and no way to tell what's current.

02 · SIGNAL

Readiness is a guess

Students study for weeks with no real sense of how prepared they are relative to what recruiters actually ask.

03 · CONTINUITY

Knowledge doesn't survive graduation

Interview patterns, recruiter feedback and hard-won prep strategies leave with every batch instead of compounding year over year.

Role-based tracks

Every track is built around a role.

Each track combines the same core subjects, weighted and sequenced for what that role's interviews actually test.

How it works

A structured path, start to interview.

STEP 1

Pick a track

Choose the role you're recruiting for. The platform sets a difficulty ladder for that track's subjects.

STEP 2

Work through modules

Short concept lessons, easy to advanced, each closing with an application-based quiz.

STEP 3

Practice real questions

Draw from a bank of interview questions sourced from prior placement cycles, tagged by topic and difficulty.

STEP 4

Check your readiness

See a topic-wise and role-wise readiness score, benchmarked against prior successful candidates, with gaps called out.
